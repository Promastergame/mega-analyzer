# Support the project

[Русский](DONATION.ru.md)

The current Mega-Analyzer version is **free to use** under
[BSD-3-Clause](../LICENSE) OR [GPLv2](../COPYING), at your option. Donations are voluntary.

New major versions will be paid upgrades. You can keep using a downloaded free
version without buying an upgrade. [Upgrade terms](SUPPORT.md#versions-and-upgrades).

## Why donate?

If Mega-Analyzer saved you time or taught you something, donations help:

- Pay for CI/CD build infrastructure
- Fund compute to train the offline GNN/HDC models
- Support development and maintenance

## How to support

- **DonationAlerts** → [donationalerts.com/r/promastergame](https://www.donationalerts.com/r/promastergame)

To commission a feature, integration or other development work, discuss the task
with the author first: [custom development](SUPPORT.md#custom-development).
A donation alone does not purchase a paid version, pay for specific work or
establish a delivery date.
